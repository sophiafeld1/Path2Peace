Path to Peace — Brand Palette
==============================

Defined as Tailwind v4 tokens in site/src/app/globals.css. Reach for the
token, not a raw hex — the role names below capture what each color is for.

NEUTRALS
  ivory     #F8F3EA   page background
  surface   #FCF8F0   card / surface
  ink       #34313A   headings, nav, charcoal dark sections
  body      #4A4753   body copy (slightly softer than ink)

PRIMARY TEAL  (brand + CTAs)
  teal-50   #ECF6F8   very soft section tint
  teal-100  #D8EDF1   pill bg / hover surface
  teal-200  #B9DEE4   powder-blue soft section accent
  teal-300  #8FC7CE   focus ring; squiggle on powder backdrop
  teal-500  #4FA3AD   primary brand / CTA fill
  teal-600  #3F8B94   CTA hover, primary link, photo ring on cool backdrop
  teal-700  #2E6970   strong link hover; "dark blue" text on warm/light bg

WARM ACCENT
  caramel       #D8A76F   photo ring + squiggle on warm bg, italic emphasis,
                          hero box frame
  caramel-soft  #EAC9A4   softer caramel for fills / hovers

BOLD ACCENT  (sparing — reserved)
  mustard   #E5A400   use only when something truly needs to shout

HEADER
  banner-bg   #DAE2E9   top banner background
  banner-line #9B9E78   banner gold rule + Contact Us button

SECTION
  section-blue  #D0DCE8   soft content band — between banner-bg and teal-200

RULES
-----

1. Decorative accents (photo rings, CurvyUnderline squiggles) match the
   temperature family of what's directly behind them, with a slight darker
   contrast inside the family. Mixing families clashes — caramel on a blue
   sky reads as a complementary collision, not warmth.
       Warm backdrop  → caramel
       Cool backdrop  → teal-300 (squiggle) / teal-600 (ring)

2. Primary CTA hover stays inside the teal scale: teal-500 → teal-600.
   Hovers never jump to charcoal or out of the brand family.

3. Form focus is brand teal (teal-500 border + teal-300 ring),
   not an accent color.

4. Dark surfaces (footer, image-less hero) use ink (#34313A), not pure black.

5. "Dark blue text" on a warm or light backdrop → teal-700 (the palette's
   deepest blue-leaning teal). Plain body copy → body. Headings → ink.
