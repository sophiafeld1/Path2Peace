# Fees & Photos

## Insurance & Payment
Self-pay basis; does not bill insurance directly. As licensed social workers, sessions may qualify for reimbursement. Monthly statements (superbills) provided upon request for out-of-network coverage submission.

## Session Fees
$185-190 per 55 minute session. Fee varies by therapist.

## Payment Methods
Cash, debit cards, checks, credit cards, and HSA/FSA cards accepted.

## Cancellation Policy
24 hours notice required. Full session fee charged for late cancellations.
