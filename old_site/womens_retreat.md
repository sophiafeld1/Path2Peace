# Women's Retreat

## Overview
A soulful sanctuary for those who hold space for others - therapists, yoga teachers, energy workers, coaches, and caregivers of all kinds. A 5-day wellness experience focused on renewal rather than intensive therapeutic work.

## Target Audience
Women in helping professions who need personal restoration and community connection.

## What's Offered
Shared practices, nourishing meals, meaningful conversations, and moments of stillness. Participants can expect to rest, reconnect with their bodies and spirits, and engage in reflection within a supportive community.

## Important Note
This is "not a space for acute emotional processing or deep therapeutic work."
