# Our Approach

## Core Philosophy
Integrative psychotherapy in Reston combining Internal Family Systems (IFS), Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR), Ego-state Therapy, somatic therapy and mindfulness techniques to address trauma through multiple perspectives.

## Methods

### Internal Family Systems & Ego-state Therapy
Treatment "explores the individual's internal landscape" by helping clients recognize various internal aspects of themselves, fostering self-awareness without judgment.

### EMDR
Uses "guided bilateral stimulation" to help clients reprocess traumatic memories and reduce their emotional impact.

### Mindfulness
"Grounding clients in the present moment" through techniques like breath work and body awareness helps reduce anxiety and create space for personal discovery.
