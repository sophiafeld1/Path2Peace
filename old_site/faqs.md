# FAQs

## Q1: What types of therapy do you offer?
EMDR Therapy, IFS therapy, Person-Centered Therapy, Parts Work, Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy, Somatic Psychotherapy, Emotionally Focused Individual Therapy, DBT, Holistic Therapy.

## Q2: Do you offer in-person or virtual sessions?
Both in-person sessions at our Reston office and telehealth options are available per client preference.

## Q3: What issues do you specialize in?
Anxiety, depression, developmental and attachment trauma, PTSD, C-PTSD, relationship issues, life transitions, gender issues.

## Q4: Do you accept insurance?
We operate out-of-network but provide monthly superbills with necessary details for clients to submit to insurance for reimbursement.

## Q5: What are your fees?
55 minute session fees range from $175-190 for both in-person at our office in Reston and telehealth. Group therapy costs vary.

## Q6: How do I schedule an appointment?
Contact Robin Feldman at (703) 340-6920 for a free phone consultation or complete the contact form on the website.

## Q7: What should I expect in the first session?
Initial sessions focus on building connection and trust with emphasis on listening and helping clients feel understood, rather than diving into deep work immediately.

## Q8: How long is each session, and how often will we meet?
Sessions typically last 55 minutes; weekly meetings recommended for trauma and anxiety. EMDR may use extended sessions for effectiveness.

## Q9: What is your cancellation policy?
Full session fees apply if cancellation notice isn't provided at least 24 hours in advance.

## Q10: Are my sessions confidential?
Confidentiality is protected except when required by law: serious harm risk, suspected abuse, or court orders.
