# Internal Family Systems (IFS)

## Definition
A therapeutic approach that sees the mind as made up of different parts, like a family. These represent various aspects of ourselves such as our inner critic, sadness, or protective instincts.

## Core Components (Dr. Richard Schwartz, founder)

### Managers
Parts attempting to maintain control and avoid emotional distress, often appearing as perfectionism or anxiety.

### Exiles
Components carrying trauma or painful memories, holding intense emotions like shame and fear.

### Firefighters
Parts activated when exiles are triggered, working to soothe pain through sometimes impulsive behaviors.

### The Self
The compassionate, centered core believed capable of leading the internal system once accessed.

## Goals
Help individuals "resolve inner conflicts, heal past traumas, and achieve a greater sense of self-awareness and emotional well-being." Rather than eliminating parts, IFS emphasizes understanding and listening to them so they can release extreme roles and feel secure, enabling greater balance and harmony.
