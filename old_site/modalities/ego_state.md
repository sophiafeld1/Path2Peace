# Ego State Therapy

## Definition
Views the personality as made up of distinct "ego states," or emotional "parts," each representing different thoughts, emotions, and behaviors. Addresses conflicted or dissociated states, particularly following trauma, by enhancing internal communication and resolving conflicts to foster emotional healing.

## Clinical Applications
Treats dissociation, complex PTSD, and childhood attachment disruptions.

## Historical Origins
Developed by psychotherapists John G. Watkins and Helen Watkins, who specialized in hypnosis, dissociation, and multiple personality work.
