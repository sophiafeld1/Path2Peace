# Robert (Bobby) Alexander, LMSW

## Professional Information
Licensed Social Worker
Location: Reston, Virginia (in-person); serves Maryland, DC, and Virginia (telehealth)

## Specializations
Serves adults 18+ and addresses "anxiety, depression, trauma, substance use, relationship concerns, self-esteem, and career or life transitions." Inclusive of LGBTQ+ clients, specifically supporting "gay, lesbian, bisexual and questioning" individuals. His prior work in digital advertising, marketing, and entrepreneurship informs his understanding of career pressures and life transitions.

## Training & Credentials
- MSW from Silberman School of Social Work, Hunter College, NYC
- Internal Family Systems (IFS) training from IFS Institute
- EMDR Therapy & Parts Work for Complex Trauma certification
- Integrative Somatic Parts Work (Levels 1 & 2)
- EMDR Basic Training
- Licensed in Virginia, Maryland, and DC

## Therapeutic Approach
Trauma-informed, supportive approach integrating Internal Family Systems, EMDR, and somatic work. Incorporates breathing exercises to develop self-regulation skills and body awareness.

## Background
Born in Australia, raised in New York City, with work experience in California. Professional background includes digital advertising, marketing, and entrepreneurship. Personal interests include yoga, meditation, cooking, baking, gardening, and exploring new destinations.

Note: Practices under supervision of Chelsea Nicole, LCSW.
