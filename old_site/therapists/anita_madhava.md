# Anita Madhava, MA

## Professional Role
Holistic therapist at Path to Peace Psychotherapy in Reston, Virginia. Resident in Counseling licensed in Virginia. Works with children, parents, adolescents, and adults.

## Credentials & Education
- Master's degree in Clinical Mental Health Counseling
- Bachelor's degree in Psychology
- Advanced certificate in Yoga and Meditation, with specialization in Cancer Yoga and Yoga Nidra
- Certified Yoga and Meditation Teacher

## Therapeutic Specializations
- Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)
- Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT)
- Somatic approaches
- Mindfulness practices
- Relational work
- Nervous system–focused practices
- Yoga and meditation

## Clinical Focus Areas
Stress, anxiety, depression, grief, trauma, life transitions, and disconnection from self or others.

## Approach Philosophy
Client-centered treatment emphasizing "listening with genuine care and unconditional positive regard." Believes healing is possible when clients feel emotionally and neurologically safe to be understood without judgment. Her style is described as "gentle, grounded, and present," with compassionate yet honest engagement.

## Additional Experience
Education sector work, Crisis Text Line volunteer, chair yoga instruction for seniors, oncology yoga, and mindfulness-based practices.

## Personal Background
Northern Virginia native and mother of three. Interests include art galleries, writing, music, nature, and tongue drum.
