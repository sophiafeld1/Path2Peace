# Robin Feldman, LCSW - Director & Therapist

## Professional Profile
Founder of Path to Peace Psychotherapy. Over 20 years in private practice specializing in anxiety disorders, trauma, PTSD, developmental and attachment trauma, with particular expertise in adoption-related issues.

## Therapeutic Approach
Collaborative with a focus on building on client's core strengths and inner resources. Meets clients "where they are, providing encouragement and support without being pushy." Treats teens and adults, emphasizing authentic relationships, warmth, and trust before advancing therapeutic work. Balances compassion with appropriate humor and teaches coping strategies for emotional regulation.

Integrates somatic modalities including EMDR Therapy, Ego State Therapy, Internal Family Systems (IFS), and AEDP, alongside sand-tray play and mindfulness techniques.

## Key Quote
"I treat teens and adults suffering from worry, pain, and fear, often stemming from early traumatic experiences, losses and/or complicated grief."

## Credentials & Training
- License: Virginia, Maryland, and West Virginia (telehealth only)
- Certification: EMDR (completed over 20 years ago)
- Education: Master's degree in Social Work from Simmons College School of Social Work (now Simmons University)
- Professional Affiliations: NASW, EMDRIA, Therapy First

## Personal Background
Married 20+ years with two daughters in college. Hobbies include hiking, kayaking, yoga, reading, traveling, and dancing.
