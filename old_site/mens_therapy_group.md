# Men's Therapy Groups

The practice now offers two men's groups. The previous single `/menstherapygroup` page is gone; these are at `/finding-traction` and `/tolerating-emotions-building-connections`.

## Group 1: Finding Traction

### Overview
A men's therapy group for men in their 20s who feel stuck or uncertain about their direction. "Feeling stuck, burned out, or unsure about your next move? You're not alone."

### Details
- Format: Weekly 90-minute sessions over 8 weeks
- Group size: Maximum 8 participants
- Start date: Late May (flexible)
- Location: In-person or virtual options
- Eligibility: DC, Maryland, or Virginia residents only
- Cost: $80 per session, superbill available for insurance reimbursement

### How to Join
Express interest through the Google Form link on the page.

## Group 2: Tolerating Emotions, Building Connections

### Overview
A weekly men's group providing "a safe, grounded, judgment-free space to explore self-awareness, emotional regulation, and the powerful connection between body and mind." Designed for men dealing with connection issues, anxiety, trauma, and loneliness.

### Focus Areas
- Healthier coping mechanisms
- Grounding and somatic awareness techniques
- Emotional resilience
- Self-confidence
- Meaningful connection and reducing isolation

### Facilitator
Led by a trauma specialist.

### Start Date
Launches in May.

### How to Join
Complete the interest form to learn more or join.
